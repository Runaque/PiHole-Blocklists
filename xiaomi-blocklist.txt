! Title: Xiaomi Blocklist
! Description: Blocks Xiaomi ads (MSA / MIUI System Ads), analytics and tracking
!              on Xiaomi phones (MIUI / HyperOS) and Xiaomi Mi Box / Mi TV devices.
!              Account, Find Device, MIUI updates and Mi Home are NOT blocked.
! Format: Adblock-style (||domain^ also blocks all subdomains). Pi-hole v6 compatible.
! Last updated: 2026-10-25
!
! Deliberately NOT blocked (needed for account, updates and device control).
! If another list blocks them, add them to your Pi-hole allowlist:
!   account.xiaomi.com, update.miui.com, update.intl.miui.com,
!   global.market.xiaomi.com, find.api.micloud.xiaomi.net, api.io.mi.com
!
! Xiaomi uses region-specific tracking hosts (tracking.<region>.miui.com) and
! adds new regions over time. For full coverage, also add this REGEX deny rule
! in Pi-hole (Domains > Regex filter), since adlists cannot hold regex:
!   ^tracking\..+\.miui\.com$
!
! Mi Box / Android TV note: home-screen "sponsored" rows come from Google's
! Android TV launcher, not Xiaomi. See the Android TV section at the bottom.
