! Title: Android Game Ads Blocklist
! Description: Blocks the third-party ad networks and mediation SDKs used by
!              mobile games: the networks AdMob/LevelPlay/MAX mediate to
!              (Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource, Liftoff/Vungle, Mintegral,
!              Pangle, InMobi, Meta Audience Network, Chartboost, and others).
! Format: Adblock-style (||domain^ also blocks all subdomains). Pi-hole v6 compatible.
! Last updated: 2026-10-25
!
! WHAT TO EXPECT
!  - Banner and interstitial ads stop loading in most games.
!  - "Watch an ad for a reward" buttons will fail or disappear.
!  - A few games refuse to start or hang on loading when their ad SDK can't
!    initialise. Fix: allowlist that network's domain for the game's device,
!    or put kids' tablets/phones in a Pi-hole group that uses this list.
!  - Ads served from the game's own servers can't be blocked by DNS.
!
! Google AdMob is NOT in this list: use google-ads-mobile-blocklist.txt
! alongside it, which covers AdMob, DoubleClick and Firebase analytics.
