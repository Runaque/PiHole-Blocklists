! Title: LG webOS Blocklist
! Description: Blocks ads, ACR (Live Plus) and ad tracking on LG webOS TVs.
!              Active entries target ads/ACR only and should not break the
!              LG Content Store, streaming apps or firmware updates.
!              Optional telemetry/update entries are commented out at the bottom:
!              remove the leading "! " to enable them, knowing what may break.
! Format: Adblock-style (||domain^ also blocks all subdomains). Pi-hole v6 compatible.
! Last updated: 2026-10-25
!
! Note: YouTube in-app ads are served from the same domains as the video and
!       cannot be blocked by DNS. Also disable IPv6 on the TV if your Pi-hole
!       does not answer AAAA/IPv6 DNS, or queries may slip past it.

! ---------------------------------------------------------------
! ACR (Live Plus) - LG's partner Alphonso (e.g. prov-lg.alphonso.tv)
! ---------------------------------------------------------------
||alphonso.tv^

! ---------------------------------------------------------------
! LG ad platform - home screen ads, ad info polling, all regions
! (us., de., fr., jp., info., ad. ... are all covered)
! ---------------------------------------------------------------
||lgsmartad.com^
||us-ad-lgsmartad-com.aws-prd.net^
||lgad.cjpowercast.com.edgesuite.net^

! ---------------------------------------------------------------
! Ad endpoints on the LG apps domain
! (only the ad hosts - the rest of lgappstv.com is the app store)
! ---------------------------------------------------------------
||ad.lgappstv.com^
||ibis.lgappstv.com^
||us.ibs.lgappstv.com^

! ---------------------------------------------------------------
! Third-party ad servers seen on LG TVs
! smartclip is a European video ad network (also used for HbbTV ads)
! ---------------------------------------------------------------
||smartclip.net^
||yumenetworks.com^

! ===============================================================
! OPTIONAL - telemetry, AI and update services
! Uncomment only what you're willing to lose. Test after each change.
! ===============================================================
! Device telemetry/status. Reported to BREAK the LG Content Store.
! ||lgtvsdp.com^
! Newer webOS (10.x) telemetry endpoint. May affect store/services.
! ||nextlgsdp.com^
! Common services (e.g. eic.service.lgtvcommon.com). May affect apps.
! ||lgtvcommon.com^
! ThinQ / IoT API. Breaks ThinQ app control of the TV.
! ||api.lgtviot.com^
! AI platform services on newer webOS. Breaks AI/voice features.
! ||ngfts.lge.com^
! Firmware updates. Blocking this STOPS TV UPDATES.
! ||snu.lge.com^
