! Title: Google Ads & Mobile App Tracking Blocklist
! Description: Blocks Google ad delivery, Google/Firebase analytics and crash
!              reporting in apps. Apple endpoints live in
!              ios-ads-tracking-blocklist.txt.
!              Compiled for freemium app ad blocking.
! Format: Adblock-style (||domain^ also blocks all subdomains). Pi-hole v6 compatible.
! Last updated: 2026-10-30

!  - Google Analytics / Tag Manager / Google Ads dashboards won't load.
!  - A few websites may hang on their cookie-consent wall (fundingchoices).
