! Title: Smart TV ACR Blocklist (core)
! Description: Blocks Automatic Content Recognition (ACR), viewing-data
!              collection and ad-tracking endpoints used by smart TVs.
!              This is the "safe" tier: it targets ACR and ad pipelines and
!              should not break streaming apps, app stores or firmware updates.
! Format: Adblock-style (||domain^ also blocks all subdomains). Pi-hole v6 compatible.
! Last updated: 2026-10-25
!
! If something breaks, check the Pi-hole query log for the TV's IP and
! allowlist the specific subdomain rather than removing a whole entry.
