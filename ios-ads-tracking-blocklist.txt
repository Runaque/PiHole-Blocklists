! Title: iOS Ads & App Tracking Blocklist
! Description: Blocks Apple's own ad and analytics endpoints, plus the
!              attribution and analytics SDKs built into many iPhone/iPad apps
!              (AppsFlyer, Adjust, Branch, Kochava, Singular, Mixpanel, ...).
! Format: Adblock-style (||domain^ also blocks all subdomains). Pi-hole v6 compatible.
! Last updated: 2026-10-30
!
! USE TOGETHER WITH
!  - google-ads-mobile-blocklist.txt  -> AdMob, DoubleClick, Firebase (in iOS apps too)
!  - android-game-ads-blocklist.txt   -> Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource, etc.
!    Despite the name, games on iOS use the same ad networks, so that list
!    works for iPhone/iPad games as well.
!
! DELIBERATELY NOT BLOCKED (needed for updates, App Store and purchases):
!   mesu.apple.com, gs.apple.com, inappcheck.itunes.apple.com, xp.apple.com
!
! IPHONE SETTINGS THAT BYPASS PI-HOLE
!  - iCloud Private Relay: Pi-hole v6 blocks it by default
!    (dns.specialDomains.iCloudPrivateRelay = true), so iPhones fall back to
!    your Pi-hole on your network. Leave that setting on.
!  - "Limit IP Address Tracking" (Settings > Wi-Fi > your network) can route
!    known tracker traffic around Pi-hole. Turn it off on your home Wi-Fi.
!  - Also turn on Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > "Allow Apps to
!    Request to Track" = off. It's free and stops cross-app tracking at the source.
